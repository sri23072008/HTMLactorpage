vijay

Early Life and Family Vijay was born in Madras (now Chennai), Tamil Nadu, to filmmaker S.A. Chandrasekhar and playback singer Shoba Chandrasekhar. He had a younger sister, Vidhya, who passed away at the age of two, and his production house, V.V Productions, is named in her memory. Vijay began acting as a child artist in his father’s films, starting with Vetri (1984), which laid the foundation for his future in cinema
